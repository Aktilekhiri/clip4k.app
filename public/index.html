Questions

Why does TikTok make my video blurry?

TikTok recompresses every upload and usually keeps a lower quality than the file you sent. A high-bitrate H.264 file survives that step with fewer artifacts.

What does Clip4K do to my file?

By default the clip is re-encoded in your browser (H.264, high bitrate); with "Skip re-encoding" the picture and sound stay as they are. Clip4K then adds a copy of the audio track with tiny empty samples at the end. In our tests TikTok then showed the clip at its original quality and bitrate. TikTok does not document why, so treat it as a workaround.

What do I get for 500 som?

Lifetime access to Clip4K on 3 devices. It is a single payment, not a subscription. Clip4K uses a workaround: if TikTok changes its processing and it stops working, we will look for a new way, but we cannot guarantee one.

Is the quality guaranteed?

No. It depends on TikTok, on how you publish and on your device. The extra data can make some players or editors open the file badly. If that happens, use a normal export.

Does 120 fps make the video smoother?

Only if your source is 120 fps. For 30 or 60 fps clips the frames are repeated, so the file is marked 120 fps but motion does not get smoother.

What is the Zernio key and is it safe to paste here?

Zernio is a separate service that posts to TikTok for you. You create the key in your own Zernio account and paste it here. It is used in this browser to call zernio.com and never reaches the Clip4K server. Without "Remember" it is forgotten when you close the tab. You can delete the key in Zernio at any time. Use a computer or phone you trust.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The picture and sound are processed in your browser. To add the tail, only the technical part of the file (its tables, no picture or sound) is sent to the Clip4K server. The video itself leaves your device only if you publish it to TikTok through your own Zernio account.

Why can 4K be slow on my phone?

The video is processed by your own device. For long 4K clips use a computer, or pick 1080p.
